2026

A Novel Intrusion Detection System for Dew Computing Environments Based on an Enhanced Federated Deep Learning Model

Alireza Fadaei, Behrang Barekatain

The Journal of Supercomputing, 82, Article 172 (2026).

Research contribution: Building on my M.Sc. thesis, this work presents a lightweight 1D-CNN with server-coordinated federated training for resource-constrained dew-computing environments. It uses Edge-IIoTset and mutual-information feature selection, reporting 15-class accuracy of 97.01% (centralized) and 96.67% (federated).

Related project: Federated Intrusion Detection.
