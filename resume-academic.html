Peer-Reviewed Publication

Fadaei, A., & Barekatain, B. (2026).

A novel intrusion detection system for dew computing environments based on an enhanced federated deep learning model. The Journal of Supercomputing, 82, Article 172. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-026-08314-x
